About Us

Gourmet Delights began as a six-seat kitchen and never quite lost the habit. We cook what the season gives us, change the menu when it stops making sense, and keep the room quiet enough to hear the person across the table. Everything is prepared in-house, from the bread to the last spoonful of sauce.
